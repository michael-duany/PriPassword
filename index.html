Privacy

Only you can open it.

Your vault is encrypted on your device. Face ID, Touch ID, or your passcode opens it — nothing new to remember, nothing to hand over.

  • AES-256-GCM encryption, on device
  • Key protected by your device's security hardware
  • Auto-locks when you step away

Vault files are excluded from iCloud and computer backups.

No cloud vault

Your passwords stay yours.

No cloud copy of your vault. No account. No server holding your passwords. Your password data stays on the devices you own — and backups leave it out.

  • No account to create or sign in to
  • No tracking, analytics, or ad SDKs
  • Your vault data is never uploaded to a cloud server

No password data is stored in iCloud Keychain. Only a subscription eligibility flag is kept there.

Direct sync

Sync device to device.

Any two of your devices, side by side. No account, no cable, no cloud in the middle — you decide when to sync.

1Open Sync on both devices.
2Match the pairing code.
3Keep them close until it's done.

Manual by design. Both devices on and nearby, app open and screen awake during transfer. Each device keeps its own local copy.

Why it exists
“I use dozens of websites and apps, and almost every one needs another password.

I wanted a convenient way to use my passwords across my own devices without relying on a cloud-hosted password vault.

I also wanted another local copy of my vault on a different device. That way, syncing could serve two purposes: making my passwords available across devices and keeping an additional copy close at hand.

As AI capabilities continue to advance, cyberattacks are evolving too. This reinforced my preference for minimizing where a complete copy of my password vault needs to exist.

So I built PriPassword around a simple idea: keep your vault on your own devices and sync directly between them.”

— Michael Duan, developer of PriPassword

Meet the Developer

Built by an Engineer,
Driven by a Personal Need.

I'm Michael Duan, an independent developer with over 10 years of experience in software engineering, primarily focused on IoT products and connected devices.

Throughout my career, I've worked across different areas of software development, including mobile applications, device communication, and connected systems. I also hold a master's degree.

PriPassword grew out of a personal need. I wanted a practical way to manage and synchronize passwords across my own devices without relying on a cloud-hosted vault.
