# PriPassword Privacy Policy

**Last updated: November 1, 2026**

This privacy policy explains how PriPassword (referred to in this document as "the App" or "we") collects, uses, stores, and protects your information. PriPassword is a local-first password manager: your password vault lives only on your own devices and is protected with strong encryption. It never leaves your device and is never uploaded to any server or cloud. The App does not transmit your passwords or vault data to any external service; the only outbound communication is with Apple for purchases/subscriptions and for the eligibility marker (tied to your Apple Account), and none of that communication contains your vault data.

---

## 1. Do We Collect Your Personal Data?

We process as little personal data as possible. In fact, the App's **vault data is never transmitted to or stored on any server—it stays only on your device**; your purchase and eligibility status are handled by Apple under your Apple Account (and contain no vault data), and we collect neither. The table below shows how we relate to the data associated with you:

| Data type | Description | Collected/stored by us? |
|---|---|---|
| Vault data (passwords, notes, 2FA keys, etc.) | Stored only locally on your device and under your control | No—kept only on your device; we cannot read it and do not upload it |
| Eligibility marker (tied to your Apple Account) | Used within the App to centrally manage your "free first sync" eligibility; contains no passwords or account information and only identifies that eligibility status | No—synced by Apple across your own devices |
| In-App Purchase and subscription records | Handled by Apple; we only receive the result of whether payment/subscription is active | No—we only receive the result and never obtain your payment information |

We also never access sensitive information such as your contacts, photos, or location, and we do not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs, nor do we upload any diagnostic or usage data (including crash logs). The system frameworks the App uses (such as for local notifications) are all processed on-device. Email feedback is composed by you and sent through your own email account; the App does not read from it or upload your vault data.

---

## 2. How We Use This Information

- **Locally encrypt, store, and display** your vault data on your device;
- **Sync data between your devices** via direct device-to-device transfer, only when **you initiate it** (purpose and permissions are described in Section 3);
- **Complete purchases and subscriptions through Apple** and provide you with the features you've purchased.

---

## 3. Permissions, Data Sharing, and Third Parties

The App requests the following system permissions, and only when needed for a feature and for the clear purposes below:

- **Bluetooth**: Used for direct device-to-device transfer to sync data. Sync is initiated manually by you, is active only during a sync session, and there is no background process that stays resident nor any persistent connection; transfers use end-to-end encryption, with a fresh key generated for each sync, and never pass through any third-party server or cloud.
- **Camera**: Used only to scan QR codes when you add two-factor authentication (TOTP). The camera is enabled only while scanning; it does not take photos or save anything to your photo library.
- **Face ID / Touch ID**: Used to unlock the App and your vault, and also supports the device passcode (verified by the system). The App auto-locks when sent to the background or left idle, and data cannot be read while locked. Verification is performed by the system; the App does not store any unlock credentials (biometrics and the device passcode are both held by the system).

**We do not share your personal data with any third party, nor do we engage in any cross-app or cross-site user tracking.** The App integrates no third-party SDKs (no ads, no analytics, no social sharing, and no third-party SDKs or identifiers used for tracking). Apart from the necessary communication with Apple (in-app purchases/subscriptions via Apple, and the eligibility marker tied to your Apple Account), the App exchanges data with no third-party service.

---

## 4. Data Security

- All vault data is stored locally on your device with **strong encryption**;
- The key used for unlocking is protected by **device security hardware** and kept only on your device; it never leaves your device;
- Data files are explicitly marked to **be excluded from iCloud or iTunes backups**;
- After you copy a password, the clipboard is **automatically cleared after about 60 seconds**;
- Exporting your data to a plain-text CSV file (which includes 2FA keys) requires **biometric confirmation**—a plain-text file is readable by anyone, so please keep it safe and delete it when done;
- Because your data never leaves your device, uninstalling the App or deleting it entirely from within the App permanently deletes it; we hold no copy.

---

## 5. Data Retention

Your vault data on your device is retained until you delete it within the App or uninstall the App. We retain no vault data on any server. The eligibility marker related to your subscription or trial eligibility is used only to record whether that Apple Account has used its "free first sync," contains no vault data, and is stored with that Apple Account. Purchase records are retained by Apple under its own privacy policy.

---

## 6. Your Privacy Rights and Data Control

Your vault data is stored entirely on your own device and under your control, so you have direct control over your data: you can **export or permanently delete** all of your vault data from within the App at any time, with no need to request it from us. Records related to subscriptions and purchases are held by Apple; to manage them, please contact Apple (through your Apple Account).

---

## 7. Children's Privacy

PriPassword is available to users of all ages, and we do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected a child's information, please let us know using the contact details below and we will delete it as soon as possible.
