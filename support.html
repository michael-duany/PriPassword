Frequently Asked Questions

How do I unlock without a master password?

Unlocking relies on Face ID / Touch ID / Optic ID or your device passcode, handled by the system. The App does not set or store any "master password," so there is no master password that could be phished or leaked. If no biometric is available on the device, the device passcode is used instead.

How do I add a two-factor code (TOTP)?

While editing an entry, tap the scan icon to scan the QR code, or paste the Base32 secret directly. Supports SHA-1/256/512, 6–8 digits, and custom periods; the entry page shows the rotating code and countdown directly.

How do I export my data?

"Settings → Export CSV" (free): biometric authentication is required before export, and the exported content is a plaintext CSV (including TOTP). Anyone can read a plaintext file, so keep it safe and delete it after use.

How does sync work?

Open the "Sync" page on two devices, bring them close, and follow the on-screen prompts — under a minute. You'll need to manually verify the same 6-digit pairing code shown on both devices. Sync only starts when you tap — there's no background service and no persistent connection.

What is the difference between the free trial and the "first sync free" offer?

Cross-device sync is an App Store subscription that includes a 7-day free trial managed by Apple; the trial is free, and when it ends it automatically becomes a paid annual subscription and renews automatically, and you can cancel anytime. In addition, you can complete one free first sync as a trial — only a sync that actually completes successfully for the first time consumes the allowance; simply opening the page, canceling the pairing, or a failed connection does not consume it.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are handled by Apple: go to Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions. After canceling, you can keep using the paid period until it ends; on other devices under the same Apple Account you can restore the purchased subscription via "Restore Purchases" in the App.

What if I switch phones / lose my phone?

Your data is stored only on the device. If you've synced before, install the App on the new device and sync once to get your whole vault back; otherwise you'll need to add everything from scratch. That's why we recommend syncing regularly.

What if I can't unlock?

Unlocking is handled by the system; the App cannot bypass it. If you truly can't unlock, the only option is to delete and reinstall the App — this erases the local data, so recover it first from another device you've synced with.
